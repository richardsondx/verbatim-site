Support

Getting started

Tap the record button, confirm that everyone present has agreed to be recorded, and speak. Verbatim downloads the transcription model once (about 140 MB), then everything runs offline on your phone.

Your first transcription is free

No card required. After that, Verbatim Pro unlocks unlimited transcriptions: $4.99/week, $29.99/year, or $59.99 once for lifetime.

Common questions

Does my audio leave my phone?

No. Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone. There is no server and nothing is uploaded.

Why is the first model download so large?

The Whisper transcription model is about 140 MB. It downloads once over Wi-Fi, then the app works fully offline, including in airplane mode.

How do I assign speakers?

Tap any speaker label in the transcript to reassign that segment to a different speaker. Long-press a speaker name to rename it everywhere.

How do I export?

Open a transcript and tap Export. Choose TXT, SRT, or Markdown.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings and tap Delete All Data. This erases every recording and transcript on your device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions, then Verbatim. Apple handles all billing.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Verbatim, go to the paywall, and tap Restore Purchases.
